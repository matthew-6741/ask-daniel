Cookies Policy

Ask Danny — cookies and browser storage in plain English

Last updated: September 6, 2026

The short version

Ask Danny uses browser storage to keep the app working, remember your choices, and save guest history on your device. If you sign in, Google Firebase may also use browser storage as part of authentication. We do not use advertising cookies or sell data for targeted advertising.

If analytics are enabled, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure how the site is used. Where the law requires consent before optional analytics are stored, we will ask first.

What counts as a cookie here

A cookie is a small piece of data stored by your browser. Websites can also use similar technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage. This policy covers all of those browser-storage technologies together.

What Ask Danny uses

TypePurposeRequired?
Essential browser storage Remembers basic app state, acknowledgements, preferences, and other settings needed for the site to work normally. Yes, for some features
Guest history Saves recent material lists on your own device when you use Ask Danny without an account. No — you can clear it anytime
Authentication storage If account sign-in is enabled, Google Firebase may store authentication state so you stay signed in between pages or visits. Only if you sign in
Analytics If enabled, Google Analytics may store identifiers used to understand visits, feature usage, and site performance. No

Third-party services

Some services used by Ask Danny may set or read their own cookies or browser storage when their features are active:

  • Google Firebase — account authentication and saved data for signed-in users.
  • Google Analytics — optional anonymous usage measurement when analytics are enabled.
  • Netlify — hosts and delivers the website and serverless functions.

Those providers handle their technologies under their own privacy terms. For the broader list of providers and what data is shared with them, see our Privacy & AI Disclosure.

What we do not use

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No cross-site behavioral advertising profiles.
  • No sale of cookie or browsing data to advertisers.
  • No requirement to accept optional analytics in order to generate a material list.

Your choices

You control browser storage through your browser. You can delete Ask Danny's stored data, block cookies, use private browsing, or use a tracking blocker. Blocking all storage may sign you out, erase guest history, or stop some convenience features from working.

Clear Ask Danny data

You can use the app's Clear history control for saved guest jobs, or clear cookies/site data for diagnostechai.com through your browser settings.

Analytics

Browser privacy controls and common tracking blockers can prevent analytics requests. If we add a consent control for optional analytics, your choice there will take priority for those technologies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Ask Danny adds or removes services that use cookies or similar storage. The date at the top will change when we make a material update.
