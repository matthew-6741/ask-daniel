Privacy & AI Disclosure

DiagnostechAI — plain English, no legalese

⚠️ Where AI is used in this product

Material lists on this site are generated by artificial intelligence. When you describe a job, your description is sent to a large language model, which writes the list of parts, quantities, and aisle numbers you see.

What that means for you

  • Aisle numbers are estimates, not guarantees. Store layouts differ by location and change when stores remodel. Treat an aisle number as a strong hint, not a promise.
  • Prices are approximate and will not match your receipt exactly.
  • The AI can be wrong. It may miss a part, suggest the wrong size, or invent an item that does not exist. Items flagged with an asterisk (*) were not matched against our verified product database.
  • This is a materials tool, not repair instructions. It tells you what to buy and roughly where it sits. It does not tell you how to do the job safely or to code. For anything involving gas, electrical, structural, or permitted work, consult a licensed professional.

Parts of this website's code and written content were also produced with AI assistance.

Who we share it with

Your job description is sent to whichever AI provider builds your list. Depending on your plan, that may be:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — privacy policy
  • Google (Gemini) — privacy policy
  • OpenAI (GPT) — privacy policy
  • Groq — privacy policy

We also use Google Firebase for accounts, Google Analytics for usage stats, Netlify for hosting, and OpenStreetMap Nominatim to turn coordinates into a city name if you enable location.

We do not sell your data. We don't run ads and we don't share your job descriptions with anyone beyond the providers listed above.

Your choices

  • Skip the account entirely — use guest mode and nothing reaches our servers.
  • Decline location — the app works fine without it.
  • Delete your history — the Clear history button in the History panel wipes it.
  • Delete your account — email the address below and we'll remove your data.
  • Opt out of analytics — any standard browser tracking blocker will stop it.

Beta status

DiagnostechAI is invite-only beta software built by a student. It may break, lose data, or change without warning. Please don't rely on it as your only record of anything that matters.
