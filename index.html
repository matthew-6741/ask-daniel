Before you start

Ask Danny builds your material lists with artificial intelligence. That means it is fast and thorough, and it also means it can be wrong.

  • Aisle numbers are estimates. Layouts differ by store and change after remodels.
  • Prices are approximate and won't match your receipt exactly.
  • Items can be missed or invented. Anything marked (*) wasn't matched to our verified product database.
  • This tells you what to buy, not how to do the job. For gas, electrical, structural, or permitted work, consult a licensed professional.

What gets collected

  • What you type or photograph is sent to an AI company (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI or Groq) to build your list. Don't include personal or sensitive details.
  • Location is optional and only used to match regional stock. Decline it and everything still works.
  • As a guest, nothing is stored on our servers — your history stays in this browser. Make an account and it's saved to Google Firebase instead.
  • Anonymous usage stats are recorded via Google Analytics with your IP anonymised. We don't sell data and don't run ads.
